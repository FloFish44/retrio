How to find a scanned document on your computer

Use local OCR and content clues to find a scanned contract, invoice or letter with an unhelpful filename.

Identify likely folders

Scanners often save files in Documents, Pictures or a manufacturer-specific folder.

Search by scan date

Sort likely folders by date if you know roughly when it was scanned.

Use words on the page

OCR turns visible page text into searchable clues such as names, references and amounts.

Improve OCR results

Clear, straight and high-contrast scans are easier to recognize.

Frequently asked questions

What is OCR?

Optical character recognition converts visible text in an image into searchable text.

Does OCR always work?

No. Low resolution, handwriting and complex layouts can reduce accuracy.

Find files using your own words

Retrio only analyses the folders you choose. Your files remain on your computer.
