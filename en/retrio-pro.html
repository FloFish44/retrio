How do I get my 15 free days?

Download Retrio, open it and click “Log in”. Enter your email address, accept the terms of use, then enter the 6-digit code you receive by email. Your 15 days of Retrio Pro start immediately, no credit card needed.

What happens after the 15 days?

Five days before the end, Retrio warns you. Without a subscription you simply go back to the free version: nothing is charged. To continue with no limits, subscribe for €9.99 per month.

Can I take another trial with a different email address?

No. To prevent abuse, only one free trial is granted per Internet connection and per computer. An encrypted fingerprint of your connection is kept for this purpose, never your IP address in clear.

How do I activate Retrio Pro if I subscribe directly?

Once subscribed, open Retrio and log in with the email address used at checkout (or click “Discover” then “Check my subscription”). Pro features unlock immediately.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel at any time from the management link Stripe sends after your first payment, with no explanation or penalty.

Are my files uploaded to the Internet?

No. Analysis and search remain 100% local. Only your email address and the status of your trial or subscription are checked online.
