Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026.

The essential principle

Retrio analyses only the folders you choose. Documents, photos, filenames and paths, extracted content, indexes and search text remain on your computer and are not sent to the Retrio website.

Licence data

If you enter an email address to activate Retrio Pro, it is sent to the Retrio licence service to verify the subscription status.

Anonymous app analytics

With your explicit consent, Retrio sends anonymous technical events to PostHog: app launch, version, operating system, completed indexing, use of major features and the category of any error. No filename, path, file content, search text, OCR result or licence email is sent. You may decline or withdraw consent at any time from Privacy in the app.

PostHog processes these events on behalf of Retrio. A random installation identifier is stored locally so we can count active users without knowing their identity.

Website analytics

With your consent, the website sends pseudonymous analytics events to PostHog: pages viewed, download clicks, payment clicks and support clicks. No document, search text, email address or banking information is sent. Your choice and a random identifier are kept in local browser storage and can be cleared from your browser settings.

A download click measures intent to download. The anonymous first-launch event from the app separately measures installations that were actually opened.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of data associated with your licence address by emailing retrio.pro@gmail.com.

Security and updates

The website uses HTTPS. This policy may change as the features and service providers used by Retrio evolve; the revision date appears at the top of this page.
