Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026.

The essential principle

Retrio only analyses the folders you choose. Documents, photos, filenames and paths, extracted content, indexes and search text remain on your computer and are not sent to the Retrio website.

Licence data

If you enter an email address to activate Retrio Pro, it is sent to the Retrio licence service to verify the subscription status.

Retrio account and 15-day free trial

To start the 15-day Retrio Pro free trial, you create an account with your email address. We use it to send you a verification code, manage your trial and subscription, and contact you about your account. If you tick the matching box, we may also send you news about Retrio: you can unsubscribe at any time.

To limit abuse (one free trial per person), we keep an encrypted, irreversible fingerprint of your Internet connection and a fingerprint of your computer. Your IP address is never stored in clear. These fingerprints are used only to check that a trial has not already been used.

Payments are processed by Stripe: Retrio neither receives nor stores your bank details. To request deletion of your account and data, contact us.

Technical app analytics

Retrio sends PostHog a minimal set of technical events: app launches, version, operating system, completed indexing, use of major features and the category of a technical error. Filenames, paths, file contents, search text, OCR results and licence email addresses are never included.

A random identifier is created again on every launch and is not retained between sessions. This minimal measurement is enabled by default and can be disabled or enabled again at any time under “Privacy” in the app.

Website analytics

With your consent, the website sends PostHog pseudonymous audience events: pages viewed and clicks leading to downloads, payment or support. Documents, search text, email addresses and payment information are never included.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of data associated with your licence email by contacting retrio.pro@gmail.com.

Security and updates

The website uses HTTPS. This policy may change as Retrio and its service providers evolve; the revision date appears above.
